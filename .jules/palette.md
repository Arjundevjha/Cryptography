## 2025-05-18 - Cipher Workbench Action Swapping Pattern
**Learning:** In interactive cipher workbenches, users frequently want to decrypt calculated ciphertexts or vice-versa. Offering a single-click "Swap Input & Output" action that swaps text and toggles mode eliminates manual copy-pasting while preserving screen-reader status announcements.
**Action:** Include "Swap" companion buttons alongside "Copy" actions in bidirectional transformer workbench components.
